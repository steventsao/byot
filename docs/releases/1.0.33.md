### Not confirmed

The blank block in `AAqE8eljRs3pWq5_qSfEZ0I` and `AL-iOayl1hthD-usBql8OKE` does not appear on the iOS 26.5 simulator, with or without the change, and no iOS 27 runtime is installed here. The diagnosis is that iOS 27 honours a bottom-aligned scroll request on a transcript shorter than the screen, which pushes the rows down to the composer. That fits both screenshots, but it has not been reproduced, and no test here fails when the scroll change is reverted. The owner's phone is the check: new session, one-line prompt, watch the first message through three tool steps, then queue a follow-up.

### Known limitations

- At the largest text sizes the centred loader can draw over an error banner, the rewound card or the queue summary while the first load is in flight.
- After a failed load, a successful retry shows the fresh transcript under the old error until the remaining requests return.
- While undo, redo, compact or fork runs, send is dimmed and the reason is only in the VoiceOver hint.
- In a row narrower than about 320 pt the control row scrolls with no visible hint.
- A long transcript whose last rows are much taller than its first can open short of the latest message. This is on `main` too.
- When the control row is crowded the model shows as a chip icon, and custom agents share one icon. Names are in the pickers and in VoiceOver.
- Two new UI tests depend on fixed fixture timers, and the offline-menu test's "no Terminal" assertion cannot fail.
- The App Store screenshots in `docs/app-store` still show the two-icon toolbar and the green chip.
