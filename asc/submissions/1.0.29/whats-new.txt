Keep your work moving between your iPhone and computer:

- Unsent text, attachments, and selected files are saved on this iPhone when you switch sessions or relaunch BYOT.
- Optional notifications let you know when a turn finishes or needs your attention.
- An optional computer queue runs accepted prompts in order while BYOT is closed. Notifications and the computer queue require the BYOT companion running on your OpenCode computer.
- Returning to BYOT refreshes the conversation without interrupting a message being sent.
- Attachment imports finish before Send becomes available, and oversized selections are caught earlier.
- Fixed text alignment on the empty session screen, including larger text sizes.
