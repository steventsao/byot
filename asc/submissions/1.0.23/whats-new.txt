This update refines how BYOT looks and feels:

- Conversations use OpenCode's type: the system font for text and controls, monospace for tools and code, and more room between turns.
- Tool calls are quiet one-line rows you can tap to see input and output, and your prompt sits in a pill.
- Swipe a session left in the session list to archive it on OpenCode 1 servers.
- Sending a message folds the composer back to a single row; tap Message to expand it. Model, agent, and reasoning effort share one row, and Files moved into the + menu.
- Controls use neutral colors, and new sessions open ready to type.

OpenCode 1 and OpenCode 2 beta remain supported. Available controls depend on your server.
