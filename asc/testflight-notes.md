BYOT 1.0.43 — the conversation stays on the latest line while OpenCode works

Everything in 1.0.42 is here too. This build has one fix and no new features.

Please test:

Following a turn
• Send a prompt that makes OpenCode run a few commands and write a long answer. While it works, the screen stays on the newest line as tool steps and paragraphs arrive. "Jump to latest" does not appear.
• While it is still working, scroll up to read earlier messages. The screen stays where you put it and "Jump to latest" appears.
• Tap "Jump to latest", or scroll back down to the end by hand. The screen follows the turn again.
• A short conversation that fits on one screen stays under the header, as before.

Things that should not change
• Opening a long conversation starts at its latest message.
• "Response needed" still takes you to a permission or question.
• Check in English and in Simplified Chinese.

Known issue, also in 1.0.42
• Scrolling back into the middle of one very long turn while it is still running can keep the app busy redrawing. If the app feels slow there, tap "Jump to latest".
